# Patent Filing Guide: Indian Patent Office Forms

You have the standard forms required to file a patent in India. Since these are official government PDFs, you will need to type this information into the PDFs (or print and fill them). 

Here is your exact **Cheat Sheet** on what to write in every major section of Forms 1, 2, 3, 5, and 9 based on the invention we just drafted.

---

## FORM 1: Application for Grant of Patent
This is the main application form stating who you are and what you are patenting.

*   **1. Applicant(s):** 
    *   **Name:** Your full legal name (and any co-founders/co-inventors).
    *   **Nationality:** Indian.
    *   **Address:** Your full residential or institutional address.
*   **2. Inventor(s):** 
    *   If you are the applicant and the inventor, check "Yes" to "Are all the inventor(s) same as the applicant(s)".
*   **3. Title of the Invention:** 
    *   *Copy this exactly:* "A Computer-Implemented Method for Reconstructing Occluded Skeletal Landmarks Using Adaptive Anthropometric Scaling to Improve Temporal Rehabilitation Classification."
*   **4. Address for Correspondence:** 
    *   Your mailing address, email, and phone number.
*   **5. Priority Particulars:** 
    *   Leave blank (unless you are filing this based on an older foreign application).
*   **6. Particulars for filing Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT):** 
    *   Leave blank.
*   **7. Particulars for filing Divisional Application:** 
    *   Leave blank.
*   **8. Particulars for filing Patent of Addition:** 
    *   Leave blank.
*   **9. Declarations:** 
    *   Check "Declaration by the inventor(s)" and sign it.
*   **Signatures:** Sign and date at the bottom.

## FORM 2: Provisional / Complete Specification
This is the actual technical document that describes the invention. **You already have this!**

*   **Title of Invention:** (Same as Form 1).
*   **Applicant:** Your Name.
*   **Preamble to the description:** 
    *   If filing a Provisional Patent (recommended first step): Check "The following specification describes the invention."
    *   If filing a Complete Patent: Check "The following specification particularly describes the invention and the manner in which it is to be performed."
*   **Description / Claims / Abstract:** 
    *   Literally copy and paste the contents of the `patent_specification.md` document we just created into this section (or attach it as Annexure pages if the PDF box is too small). 
    *   If filing Provisional, you technically don't *need* claims, but including the ones we wrote makes it much stronger.

---

## FORM 3: Statement and Undertaking Under Section 8
This form simply promises the government that you will tell them if you apply for this exact same patent in another country (like the USA).

*   **1. Name of Applicant:** Your Name.
*   **2. Statement:** Check the box that says you have *not* made any application for the same invention outside India (assuming this is true).
*   **3. Undertaking:** You promise to keep the Controller informed in writing of any foreign applications you make in the future.
*   **Signatures:** Sign and date.

---

## FORM 4: Request for Extension of Time (You don't have this, which is good)
*Skip.*

---

## FORM 5: Declaration as to Inventorship
This form proves who actually invented the technology. If you invented it alone, this is straightforward.

*   **1. Name of Applicant:** Your Name.
*   **2. Inventors:**
    *   Insert your Name, Nationality, and Address. 
    *   Sign next to your name.
*   **3. Declaration to be given when the applicant in India is different from the inventor:**
    *   If you are both the inventor and the applicant, you can usually leave this blank or state N/A. 
*   **4. Statement (if claiming priority):**
    *   Leave blank.

---

## FORM 9: Request for Early Publication
Normally, the Indian Patent Office keeps your patent secret for 18 months before publishing it. If you want it published within 1 month (so you can show investors or put "Patent Published" on your resume), you file Form 9. *Note: There is a fee for this.*

*   **Applicant:** Your Name.
*   **Statement:** "I/We hereby request for early publication of my/our Patent Application No. _______ [Leave blank if submitting together, the office assigns the number] dated _______ under Section 11A(2) of the Patents Act, 1970."
*   **Signatures:** Sign and date.

---

### Next Steps for Submission:
1.  **Print and Sign:** Print these PDFs, fill in the text as guided above, and sign them physically (or use an approved Digital Signature Certificate if filing via the IPO online portal).
2.  **Attach Drawings:** Print the Mermaid Flowcharts from `patent_diagrams.md` on blank A4 paper. Label the pages "Drawing 1 of 1" etc., and attach them to Form 2.
3.  **Filing:** You can file these electronically at [ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/goForLogin/doLogin) if you have a Class 3 Digital Signature, or you can physically mail/submit them to the relevant regional Patent Office (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, or Kolkata) based on your address.
